Two lanes read the city. A five-minute lane re-checks contract awards and liquor licences and reads every record's clock; an hourly lane rebuilds the whole feed. Each request is attributed to its record, so a failure here names the source, not the run. A clock read is shown beside its record but never decides its state: only a failed read of the rows opens an incident. The five-minute figures are at most a minute old; the hourly report is committed to the repository and can be up to five minutes behind. Times are New York. /api/status is the same data as JSON, behind the same key.
